Privacy policy

This policy covers shuddhasangama.org, run by Shuddha Sangama, Bengaluru, India. Each of our products, such as DhaShu, has its own privacy policy on its own site.

What we collect

This website has no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics. We only collect what you choose to send us, such as your name, email address and the content of your message when you email us.

How we use it

We use what you send only to reply to you. We do not sell, rent or share your information for marketing.

Third-party services

Some pages show YouTube videos, embedded in privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube may set cookies once you play a video, under Google's own privacy policy. Links to LinkedIn, Amazon, SSRN and other sites are covered by those sites' policies. Our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

How long we keep it

We keep emails only as long as we need them to respond, or as the law requires.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other laws that apply, you can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you have sent us. Write to hello@shuddhasangama.org.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date above.
